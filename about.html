About

A classic soul, a modern hand.

Tatiane Borges is an interior designer specializing in residential interiors that balance classic architecture with a contemporary sensibility. Working from first concept to final styling, she pairs refined materials — marble, walnut, brushed brass — with photorealistic 3D visualization, so every decision is resolved before it is built.

Her work spans full homes and apartments: living and dining rooms, kitchens and bars, spa-like bathrooms, bespoke dressing rooms, home offices and outdoor retreats.
